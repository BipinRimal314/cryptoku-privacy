Cryptoku support

Contact

Email bipinrimal314@gmail.com. We usually reply within two working days. In the app you can also use Settings → Send Feedback.

Accounts

Cryptoku has no account or login. Your games, statistics and settings stay on your device.

Membership and refunds

Membership is sold through the App Store. To restore it on a new device, open Settings → Become a member → Restore Purchases. Apple handles refunds: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete local data erases everything Cryptoku stored on this device. To remove feedback you sent us, email us and we will delete it.

Daily reminder

Turn the daily puzzle reminder on or off in the iOS Settings app → Notifications → Cryptoku.
