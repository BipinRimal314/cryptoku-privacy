Purchases and refunds

Membership

Cryptoku Membership is an auto-renewing subscription billed monthly or yearly through your Apple Account. Playing Cryptoku never starts a paid subscription by itself.

Cancel or manage

Uninstalling an app does not cancel a subscription. Cancel in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions, or from the Manage subscription button on the membership screen. Cancelling stops future renewals; you keep access until the end of the paid period.

Refunds

Apple handles refunds, not the developer. Request one at https://reportaproblem.apple.com. Eligibility depends on the purchase, timing and applicable law; a refund is not guaranteed.

Restore

If you reinstall or change device, use Restore Purchases on the membership screen.
