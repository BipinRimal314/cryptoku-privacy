Privacy policy

Who makes Cryptoku

Cryptoku is developed by Bipin Rimal, Kathmandu, Nepal. Contact: bipinrimal314@gmail.com. No account or login is required.

Local data

Saved puzzles, lesson progress, solve statistics, age group and preferences are stored on this device. Imported puzzle links and JSON are decoded locally without fetching the linked page. Use Settings → Delete local data to erase app progress and settings. Deleting the app also removes them.

Age privacy

Cryptoku is for a general audience. We ask for your birth year, keep only an age group on your device, and never store the year. Before an age entry, and for players under 16, the app does not request ads or allow its feedback form to send data. Local puzzles remain available. This is not a parental consent service.

Advertising

For players aged 16 or older, Google AdMob provides occasional interstitial ads between puzzles and optional rewarded ads for extra answers, filling notes or opening past daily puzzles. Before any ad is requested, Google’s consent form checks whether ads may be requested. Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt asks whether the app may track you across other companies’ apps and websites; if you decline, you still see ads, but they are not personalised. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and the consent choice in Settings → Ad Privacy Choices where shown. AdMob may process device identifiers, approximate location from IP, ad interactions and diagnostic data to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. Google controls its own retention and privacy processes. Members see no ads.

Feedback

Nothing is sent until you press Send. Your message, optional email, app version, operating system version and screen context are sent through FormSubmit to the developer’s email. They are used for support, not marketing. Messages are kept while needed to address the request and related follow-up; records may be retained where needed for legal obligations. Avoid including sensitive information. Request deletion by emailing the developer; this covers records under our control. Provider records are subject to that provider’s retention rules.

Purchases

Cryptoku Membership is an auto-renewing subscription bought through the App Store with your Apple Account. Apple handles payment; we do not receive card or bank details, only whether a subscription is active on your Apple Account.

Your choices and requests

Use Delete local data for information on this device. Use Request data deletion for feedback or other information sent to the developer; no account is needed. We may ask for enough information to identify the records and verify the request. Where applicable, you can also request access or correction or contact your privacy regulator. Tracking and advertising controls in iOS Settings and Google’s privacy tools manage data held by Google. We do not sell personal data.

Changes

This policy describes the iPhone and iPad app. Material changes will be reflected in updated notices.
