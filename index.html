Cryptoku Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 18, 2026

Cryptoku ("the App") is a variant Sudoku puzzle game developed by Bipin Rimal ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the App handles, where it goes, and the choices you have.

Summary

  • We run no servers and receive none of your data. There is no account, login or email address.
  • Your games, statistics and settings stay on your device.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob, not us, collects some device data to serve and measure those ads.
  • Buying ad-free turns off ads, so the App stops requesting them.

Data Stored on Your Device

The App stores the following on your device only. It is never sent to us or anyone else:

  • Puzzle progress and saved games
  • Gameplay statistics (solve times, completion counts, streaks)
  • Lesson progress
  • Preferences (theme, sound, haptics, focus mode, note settings)
  • An optional profile picture, if you choose one from your photo library. The App only receives the single photo you pick, and keeps it on the device.
  • Puzzles you import by pasting an f-puzzles link or JSON. These are read on the device; the App makes no network request to fetch them.

Uninstalling the App deletes all of this data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the App shows two kinds of ad, and never during a puzzle:

  • An interstitial ad between some puzzles.
  • An optional rewarded ad you may choose to watch in exchange for an extra answer hint.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. When ads are shown, the AdMob software inside the App collects and shares with Google the following data:

  • Device identifiers, including your device's advertising ID
  • Approximate location, derived from your IP address (not GPS)
  • App interactions, such as ad views and taps
  • Diagnostics, such as crash and performance information about ad delivery

Google uses this data to deliver and measure ads, including personalised ads where permitted, for analytics, and to prevent fraud. It is encrypted in transit. How Google handles it is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your Choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): before any ad is requested, the App shows Google's consent form where the law requires it. You can change your answer later in Settings, under Ad Privacy Choices.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings (Android: Settings, Privacy, Ads; iOS: Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking).
  • Remove ads: buying ad-free turns off all ads in the App, and it stops making ad requests.

In-App Purchases

Ad-free is sold through Google Play Billing on Android (a one-time purchase) and Apple In-App Purchase on iOS (a subscription). Payments are processed entirely by Google or Apple. We never see or store your payment details; the App only learns whether ad-free is active on your device.

Analytics

The App contains no analytics or tracking of its own. The only data collection is AdMob's, described above.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. The App has no user-generated content, chat or social features.

Selling Data

We do not sell your data. We hold none to sell.

Changes to This Policy
