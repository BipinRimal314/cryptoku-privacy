Cryptoku Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 28, 2026

Cryptoku ("the App") is a variant Sudoku puzzle game developed by Bipin Rimal ("we", "us"). This policy describes how we handle your information.

Data We Collect

We collect no personal data. Cryptoku does not require an account, login, email address, or any personal information to use.

Data Stored on Your Device

The App stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Puzzle progress and saved game states
  • Gameplay statistics (solve times, completion counts)
  • App preferences (theme, sound, haptics settings)
  • Daily challenge streak data
  • Teaching mode progress

This data never leaves your device. We cannot access it. If you uninstall the App, this data is deleted.

In-App Purchases

Cryptoku offers a one-time premium unlock via Google Play Billing (Android) or Apple In-App Purchase (iOS). Purchase transactions are handled entirely by Google or Apple. We do not collect or store your payment information.

Third-Party Services

The App uses RevenueCat to manage in-app purchase verification. RevenueCat receives an anonymous device identifier to validate your purchase status. No personally identifiable information is shared.

Advertising

The free version of Cryptoku displays interstitial advertisements between puzzles (never during gameplay) using Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data as described in Google's Privacy Policy. You can remove all ads permanently with a one-time in-app purchase.

Analytics

The App does not use any first-party analytics or tracking. AdMob may collect anonymized usage data as part of ad delivery.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The App contains no user-generated content or social features.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share any data with third parties. There is no data to share.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, we will post the updated version at this URL and update the effective date above.
