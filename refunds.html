Purchases and refunds

Current release

No new membership or remove-ads purchase can be made in this Android release. Playing Cryptoku does not start a paid subscription.

Earlier purchases

For a purchase previously made through Google Play, review your purchase history and request a refund through Google Play. Eligibility depends on the purchase, timing, store policy and applicable law; a refund is not guaranteed.

Subscriptions

Uninstalling an app does not cancel an existing subscription. Manage or cancel any earlier subscription in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Cancellation and a refund are separate actions.
